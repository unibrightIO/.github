### ProofMatter — the Proof-of-Execution layer

[ProofMatter](https://www.proofmatter.com) proves not just that a transaction was
recorded, but that a real-world action was actually executed, validated and
auditable over time — for AI agents, training data and content provenance as
much as for ESG reporting, product passports and supply chains. Proofs are
anchored on Optimism; the business data stays with its owner. Protocol access
runs on [UBT](https://unibright.io/token.html), an ERC-20 utility token in
circulation since 2018.
